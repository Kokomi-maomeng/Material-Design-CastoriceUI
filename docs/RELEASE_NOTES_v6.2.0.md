# CastoriceUI v6.2.0

## 中文

- 流量分析将趋势、近期月流量和横向协议分布合并到一个可折叠卡片。月流量统一保留一位小数。
- 在线连接迁入流量分析下方，保留实时速率、筛选、目标和复制操作。总览快捷入口和原书签均指向新区块。
- 账号管理在首行固定未归属流量。订阅弹窗打开时先显示二维码区域和按钮，地址就绪后可复制。
- 统一总览、流量分析和网络质量图表的浮动读数、曲线和输入交互。组合输入框的焦点环覆盖整个控件。
- 审计记录可按中英文操作和详情搜索。统一界面时间单位，并保留已有审计记录及设置。

## English

- Traffic analytics groups the trend, recent monthly usage, and horizontal protocol shares in one collapsible card. Monthly values use one decimal place.
- Active connections moves beneath the analytics card and retains rates, filters, live destinations, and copying. The Overview shortcut and existing bookmarks open the new section.
- Account management pins unattributed traffic at the top. The subscription dialog reserves space for the QR code and shows its actions while the protected URL loads.
- Overview, Traffic analytics, and Network quality share tooltip styling, curve interpolation, and pointer, touch, and keyboard inspection. Focus rings follow the full shape of composite fields.
- Audit searches match Chinese and English actions and details. The interface uses consistent duration units and preserves existing audit records and settings.
